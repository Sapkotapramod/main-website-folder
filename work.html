How the maths works

Lorentz factor: gamma = 1 / sqrt(1 - v²/c²)

Speed effect on a clock: -v² / (2c²)

Gravity effect on a clock: GM/c² × (1/R - 1/(R+h))

Simplifications: the twin trip ignores the turnaround acceleration, and the calculator ignores Earth's rotation, so its numbers are close but not exact.
