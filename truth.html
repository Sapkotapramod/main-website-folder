When you continuously use Google services (such as Google Search, Google Chrome, Gmail, Google Maps, and Android), the company collects a massive, continuous stream of personal and behavioral data rather than packaging and selling your raw identity to third parties. What Data Google Collects • Active Activity Data: This includes everything you type into Google Search, the websites you visit via Google Chrome, emails sent and received in Gmail, videos watched on YouTube, and voice commands given to Google Assistant. • Location and Movement Data: If location services are enabled, Google Maps and your device log the places you visit, your travel routes, and real-time coordinates. • Passive Background Data: Studies show that a significant portion of data collection happens passively in the background (even when you are not actively interacting with a Google app), tracking device identifiers, IP addresses, screen sizes, and app usage patterns. • Profiling: All this information is stitched together to build a detailed psychological and behavioral profile used primarily to deliver targeted advertising. Does Google "Release" or Sell Your Data? • No Direct Sales: Google states in its official policy that it does not sell your personal information (name, direct contact info, or raw spreadsheets) for cash to third-party buyers. • Cross-Context Sharing (Advertising): Google "shares" or processes your behavioral data to let advertisers target specific audiences without handing over your identity directly. Advertisers pay Google to place ads in front of you based on the profile it builds. • Law Enforcement Requests: Google does release user data when legally compelled to do so via valid government or law enforcement warrants and subpoenas, which are detailed in their transparency reports. How to Control or Limit Data Collection • Review Activity Controls: Visit your Google Account Dashboard at myaccount.google.com to manage or turn off Web & App Activity, Location History, and YouTube History. • Set Auto-Deletion: You can configure your account to automatically delete your search and activity data on a rolling 3-month, 18-month, or 36-month basis. • Manage Permissions: Check the "Third-party apps" section under your account settings to revoke access for external apps connected to your Google profile.
